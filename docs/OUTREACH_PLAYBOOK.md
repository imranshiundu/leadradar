# Outreach Playbook

## Positioning

Sell one thing first:

> I build clean business websites, booking/contact pages, and WhatsApp-linked landing pages delivered fast.

## First message

Use short, honest, low-pressure outreach:

```text
Hi {Business Name},

I’m Imran. I build clean, fast websites and contact/booking pages for small businesses.

I noticed your business may not have a dedicated website yet.

If useful, I can send you two simple ideas for improving your online presence. No pressure — if this is not relevant, reply "no" and I will not contact you again.

Best,
Imran
```

## Follow-up

One follow-up only:

```text
Hi {Business Name}, just checking whether a simple website/contact page would be useful for you this month.

I can send two quick ideas first before you decide.
```

If they do not respond, stop.

## What to offer

- KES 5,000-10,000: one-page business/contact site.
- KES 10,000-20,000: website with services, gallery, WhatsApp, form.
- KES 25,000+: simple admin dashboard/content editing.

## Close script

```text
I can build the first version in 48 hours.

To start, I need:
1. Business name
2. Services/products
3. Logo if available
4. 5-10 photos if available
5. WhatsApp number
6. Preferred colors

Deposit: 50%.
Balance: after review before final handover.
```
